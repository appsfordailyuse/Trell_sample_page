Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before purchasing your building.

How do I purchase a building? ▼
Purchasing is quick and hassle-free — everyone is approved and no credit check is required. Just bring a valid ID, your down payment, and two references (name, address, phone — must not live with you). Once completed, we schedule your delivery or place your custom order.
Do you offer financing? ▼
Yes! We offer flexible Rent-To-Own plans with 24, 36, 48, 60, and 72-month terms. You can also pay off your balance within 90 days with absolutely no interest or fees. No credit check required — ever.
What are the approval requirements? ▼
Getting approved is simple. You only need: a valid government-issued ID, a down payment, and two references that don't live with you. No credit check, no income verification, no hidden requirements.
How long does delivery take? ▼
In-stock buildings are typically delivered within 3–10 business days depending on your location, weather, and site readiness (cleared and level). Custom orders may take longer. We'll give you an estimated timeline upfront.
Can I pay off my building early? ▼
Absolutely! You can pay off your building early at any time and save money by eliminating future rental fees. There are no early payoff penalties and no hidden charges. Pay off within 90 days and owe zero interest.
What customization options are available? ▼
Trell buildings are completely customizable. Add windows, extra doors, shutters, shelves, transom windows, dormers, and lofts. Choose metal or shingle roofing, and select from wood, metal lap, Z-metal, or painted siding — all at no extra cost for standard options.
Do you deliver outside Louisiana? ▼
Yes! Trell serves all of Louisiana and also delivers to highlighted surrounding states. Contact us to confirm delivery availability and pricing for your specific area.
What warranty is included? ▼
Trell Portable Buildings offers a 5-year limited warranty on materials and workmanship for our standard product line, and a 3-year limited warranty on our Best Value line. We stand behind every building we build. Guaranteed satisfaction or your money back.
